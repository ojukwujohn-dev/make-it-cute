Make It Cute privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Make It Cute is a DIY craft app for kids, tweens and teens. It was designed and created by Charice, a young crafter, and is published by her dad. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, sign-ups, ads, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking. The app never asks for a real name, email, address, school, age or location.

What stays on the device

To remember progress, the app saves pins, finished crafts, settings, designs, a nickname and picture choice, photos and videos in the device's own storage. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it unless a save code or backup file was made.

Camera and photos

The app only uses the camera or photo library when the user taps to add a photo or film a craft video. Those photos and videos stay on the device. They are only shared if the user chooses to save or send them outside the app.

Save codes and sharing

A save code, QR code or backup file is created on the device and goes only where the user puts it.

Crafter Showcase

Showcase videos are chosen and checked by the parent who runs the app. There is no way to upload from inside the app. A featured crafter is shown by first name or nickname only, and only with their parent's permission.

Other services

The Android and iPhone apps have everything built in and don't connect to any server. The website version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website host, may process basic technical information such as an IP address so the page can load. We don't receive or use that information. The app uses no other third-party services or SDKs.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side. This is in line with Google Play's Families policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
