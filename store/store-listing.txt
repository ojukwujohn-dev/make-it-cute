APP NAME (30 max)
Make It Cute: DIY Crafts

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
100 cute DIYs for kids: squishies, crochet, bows, treats, nail art & room decor.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Charice designed and created Make It Cute when she was 10, with help from her dad. It's a craft app made by a young crafter, for crafters like her. Every tutorial uses simple supplies you probably already have at home.

✦ 100 step-by-step tutorials: slow-rise and paper squishies, crochet, bows and hairstyles, jewelry, sock plushies, bookmarks, no-bake treats, baking, nail art, spa nights, journaling, bedazzling, charms, sleepover kits and room decor.
✦ Design studio: Squishy Designer, Bracelet Maker, Bookmark Designer, Door Hanger Maker and Paper Duck Dress-Up.
✦ Printable templates for paper squishies, door hangers and bookmarks.
✦ "What can I make?" lets you tick what you have and shows crafts you can make right now.
✦ Room Makeover sets for coquette, cottagecore, Y2K and more.
✦ Pin crafts, earn "made it" stamps and build a shopping list.
✦ A private video diary for filming your crafts.
✦ Read-aloud steps, bigger text, calm mode and a "no sharp stuff" setting.

Safe by design:
• No ads, no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases
• Collects no personal information. Everything stays on your device
• Grown-up tips and "heads up" safety notes on every craft that needs them
• Works offline
